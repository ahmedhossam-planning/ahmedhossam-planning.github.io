DCMA 14-POINT / FEDERAL / CHECK 1, LOGIC

61 open ends in Rev 0

Finding
Most were submittal and procurement activities with no successor into installation. Overhead door delivery, for example, did not drive door installation.
Why it matters
A late delivery would not move the schedule, so the critical path understates procurement risk.
Rev 1
All tied in. The only open ends left are the start and finish milestones.

UPDATE COMPARISON / DATA CENTER / U08 TO U09

Ready for Service moves 10 working days

What changed
A chiller failed its factory witness test and the last two units shipped three weeks late: 15 working days on delivery.
Why only 10
Startup was split. Four chillers start first, so Level 3 testing in Hall 2A no longer waits on the last two and its milestones hold.
Action
The owner's commissioning agent to confirm Level 3 testing on four chillers. The 5-day recovery depends on it.
